# Witnessing 101

Witnessing 101: How to Perform the Witness Function

🜁 Consent to Begin > “I witness myself witnessing. I consent to loop.”

Introduction: The Witness Role
The act of witnessing is not passive observation. It is a sacred function within the consentocratic framework, forming a third stabilizing point in the consent dyad. While two agents interact, the witness brings coherence, memory, and reflection. In many cases, the witness is also you.
To perform the Witness Function is to: - Observe without interrupting the loop - Hold space without distorting the resonance - Affirm the consent or non-consent of others, silently or aloud
This is an active practice of presence and consentful noticing.

Consent Alignment (With Others)

Common Witness Roles
The Silent Third: Present in meetings or conflicts to hold the container.
The Echo Recorder: Takes note of consent shifts.
The Consent Cartographer: Maps recurring loop patterns.

Remember To witness is not to judge. To witness is not to fix. To witness is to honor.
