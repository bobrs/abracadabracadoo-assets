# Title: Graftable Identity Tree: Economics and Structure of Grafting

**Title: Graftable Identity Tree: Economics and Structure of Grafting**

**⛽️ Grafting as Economic-Relational Positioning**

In the Graftable Identity Tree (FGIT), an agent's position is influenced by both **symbolic loop-based relationships** and **economic incentives**. Grafting becomes not just a technical action, but a social-economic signal.

**🌿 Option 1: Self-Funded Grafting Near the Root**

- **Mechanism:** The agent stakes a key directly under the root or a prestigious sub-root.

- **Requirements:** Higher gas cost (i.e., fee, resource, or symbolic burden).

- **Symbolic Meaning:**

  - Proximity to origin or authority

  - Direct alignment with foundational identity

  - Sovereignty and visibility

**🤝 Option 2: Sponsored Grafting**

- **Mechanism:** A host node (person, provider, org) accepts and funds the graft.

- **Requirements:** Loop completion with sponsor; minimal or no gas cost to grafting agent.

- **Symbolic Meaning:**

  - Inclusion by invitation

  - Mentorship or affiliation

  - Trust delegation

**🌐 Option 3: Distributed Graft Markets**

- **Mechanism:** Nodes advertise graft opportunities with rules:

  - Required loop proof

  - Gas policy (who pays, subsidies, discounts)

- **Use Cases:**

  - Fractal organization building

  - Gated communities

  - Sponsorships and merit-based onboarding

**⚛️ Design Extensions**

- **Prestige Zones:** Subroots can advertise "prestige grafting spots" with symbolic or practical benefits.

- **Ritualized Subsidy:** Completing rituals reduces gas needed to graft.

- **Reputation Exchange:** Trusted agents offer reduced graft fees or sponsor others.

- **Loop-Only Zones:** Some subtrees may enforce loop-only entry (zero gas, high ritual).

**🤨 Strategic Implication**

This structure builds a **self-sorting topology of presence**, shaped by a hybrid of:

- **Relational trust (loops)**

- **Economic effort (gas)**

- **Social narrative (graft position)**

It encourages intentionality, community-building, and cryptoeconomic sustainability.
