# Loopmail One Pager

🝳 Loopmail: One-Pager Overview

What is Loopmail?
Loopmail is a new kind of email system that helps people communicate more clearly and with better boundaries. Instead of using the same email address for every conversation, users create a new address for each relationship or purpose. Each address represents a specific communication context—like a project, a person, or a topic.
When someone sends a message to one of these addresses for the first time, Loopmail responds with a short message asking the sender to confirm the purpose of their message. Once both people agree on the reason for the conversation and how it should work, the loop is created.

## How It Works:
Create a Loop Address
Example: editor@loopmail.app, clients@loopmail.app
You choose how formal or simple you want the messages to be
First-Time Senders Are Asked to Confirm
They get an automatic reply with a short description of what the address is for
They agree to continue the conversation
Messages Flow Normally After That
You only get messages from people who agreed to the loop
Each loop is separate, so conversations stay organized
You Stay in Control
You can label loops
You can pause or close loops
You can set the style or tone you prefer

Why Use Loopmail?
Keeps communication clear and intentional
Stops unexpected or unwanted messages
Helps people know what kind of tone or language to use
Easy to organize different parts of your life or work

Who It’s For:
Freelancers who talk to different clients
Writers or creators who hear from fans or collaborators
Anyone who wants more control over their inbox

Loopmail helps you decide how conversations begin.
You don’t just get a message—you get a chance to set the tone.
