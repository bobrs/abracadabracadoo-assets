# Loop-Based Consent Infrastructure for Healthcare & Mental Wellness

Loop-Based Consent Infrastructure for Healthcare & Mental Wellness
Building Trust, Memory, and Presence into the Healing Process

🌱 Vision
The therapeutic process thrives on trust, timing, and intentionality—yet today’s systems are optimized for billing, compliance, and surveillance. The Loopverse platform introduces a new layer: a consent-driven, memory-aware infrastructure that supports human connection with privacy and symbolic depth.
Using lightweight cryptographic rituals and memory-bearing tokens, we enable caregivers, therapists, patients, and families to create shared moments that are:
Verifiable (via time-synced presence)
Mutual (via explicit, reversible consent)
Meaningful (via symbolic payloads)

🔧 How It Works
TOTP Consent Loops: Secure, time-bound pairings that confirm mutual presence and shared intention
NFC Tokens & Charms: Physical objects that carry encrypted memory fragments, affirmations, or therapeutic rituals
ESP32 Anchors: Simple ritual stations (e.g., at clinics, homes, or support groups) that bind moments into portable tokens
Synaptic Filters: Optional modulation of digital interactions (e.g., reminders, journaling) based on emotional trust tone

💡 Sample Applications
1. Therapy Session Tokens
After a session, the therapist and client loop-in a memory token that captures a shared commitment, boundary, or ritual. The token can:
Unlock a reflection prompt in the client’s app
Glow or pulse during moments of need
Remind both parties at next session of progress milestones
2. Caregiving & Boundaries
Consent loops between family members, care workers, and elders establish clear temporal boundaries:
“You’re my caregiver from 3–6pm today” → verified by ritual handshake or token tap
Deactivates automatically at loop expiry
3. Grief & Support Rituals
Tokens distributed at funerals or support groups can hold encrypted voice notes or memory glyphs:
Only unlockable when re-looped with another group member
Acts as a gentle prompt to reconnect, reflect, or remember
4. Anxiety Grounding Tools
Loop charms that activate breathing guides, audio glyphs, or personal affirmations when held or tapped
Reinforced via trusted therapist-client loop memory
Integrates with mobile app via looptloop.io SDK

🌐 Platform Access: looptloop.io
APIs for loop creation, pairing, and memory tokenization
SDKs for integration into mental health platforms or provider portals
Privacy-first: all loops are ephemeral unless consented to be stored
