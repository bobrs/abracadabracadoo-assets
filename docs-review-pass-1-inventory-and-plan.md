## Must Not Accidentally Collapse

- Do not collapse Abracadoo.app into the entire Abracadabracadoo protocol ecosystem; it is the local-first reference implementation, not the whole field.
- Do not collapse HumanKey into storage, vault, transport, policy, image carriers, servers, Nostr, or manual exchange; those are adapters/carriers/embodiments.
- Do not collapse Path into transport. A Path is a one-way route for app-native trust or communication; QR/NFC/photo/manual/server/Nostr are carriers.
- Do not collapse private invite Paths with public/broadcast/listening paths; KeyCast, Field Echo, Wave-State, and directories need separate visibility semantics.
- Do not collapse Relationship into TOTP verification. TOTP proves possession/session authentication; current app Relationship requires witnessed reciprocal exchange/completed Loop.
- Do not collapse Acquaintance into Relationship. An Acquaintance is a one-way local verification possibility until reciprocal evidence completes the loop.
- Do not collapse sent/received witness events into permanent public records; storage/forgetting and declaration layers decide retention and visibility.
- Do not collapse a loop witness event into a witness-readable artifact; Addendum III and V-A imply different structures and disclosure powers.
- Do not collapse proof of delivery/receipt into consent to content; the Consent Confirmation Loop extension exists because those are different acts.
- Do not collapse deletion into universal erasure; storage/forgetting defines official loss of capability, not deletion from the universe.
- Do not collapse controlled verifiability into permanent nonrepudiation; conditional deniability and signature-block release preserve participant control.
- Do not collapse TIS semantics into the internal app ontology; TIS is best treated first as an export/interoperability layer.
- Do not collapse FractalIdentity identity channels/personas into HumanKey Relationships; they are roadmap identity contexts, not current authentication facts.
- Do not collapse Loopmail tone/purpose/context metadata into base proof validity; it may enrich messages without defining whether a Relationship exists.
- Do not collapse group/quorum/nested loops into two-Path Relationships; group semantics need separate collapse and quorum models.
- Do not collapse wall-clock TOTP windows with subjective epochs; HK_TOTP_1 can remain concrete while reserving later loop-local time semantics.
- Do not collapse AI summaries, evaluations, or recommendations into messages; Addendum VII treats downstream interpretation as derived artifacts with separate consent questions.
- Do not collapse object/device/LOOPtLOOP pairings into human relationships; presence adapters can verify nouns without granting Relationship status in the HumanKey ontology.
